The concept

Not exactly a restaurant. Nor a wine bar.

Cicchetti started as a Venetian habit — a glass of wine, a few bites, standing up, no reservation required. We kept the habit and lost the rules. Fresh pasta, wood smoke, and whatever the sea gave us this morning, served the way Tel Aviv actually eats: a little at a time, with everyone reaching across the table.

A bit of booze, a lot of soul.

Avi Kashi, Host & Cocktail Director
