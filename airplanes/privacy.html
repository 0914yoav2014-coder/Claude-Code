Privacy policy

Draft. Plain-language starting point; have it reviewed before launch and add the operator's name and contact address.

What we collect

Only your email address, and only if you type it into the sign-up form. We don't ask for your name, age, location or anything else.

Why

To send you one email a week about an airplane. We never sell or share your address, and we never send it to advertisers.

Kids under 13

If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign up. The form asks you to confirm this. If a parent finds that their child signed up without permission, contact us and we'll delete the address.

Unsubscribe and deletion

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes your address from the list. You can also ask us to delete it at any time.

Analytics

We count clicks on the Start exploring button, use of the globe and sign-ups so we can make the page better. These counts are not linked to your email address.
